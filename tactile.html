Soft Tactile Sensor Research

UC Santa Cruz · Tactile Manipulation Lab

Overview

I developed soft tactile sensors for robotic manipulation in Professor Tae Myung Huh’s Tactile Manipulation Lab at UC Santa Cruz. The sensor uses a dissolvable 3D printed PVA scaffold to create a diamond lattice of internal channels inside soft silicone.

My work

I designed the sensor geometry, made molds and sacrificial PVA scaffolds, fabricated silicone samples, tested conductive carbon black silicone and ionic hydrogels, and measured electrical responses to pressure.

Project focus

A single-side wiring exit keeps the sensing surface flexible. I explored how diamond lattice channels, channel width, and conductive layer thickness affect touch response and sensor performance.
