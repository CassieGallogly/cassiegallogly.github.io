Soft Tactile Sensor Research

UC Santa Cruz · Tactile Manipulation Lab

Overview

Designed and fabricated a flexible tactile sensor for robotic manipulation. The design uses a sacrificial 3D-printed PVA scaffold to form diamond lattice channels inside soft silicone. I tested conductive materials and explored how sensor geometry affects electrical response to applied pressure.

My work

Sensor design, CAD, silicone fabrication, PVA scaffolds, conductive materials, experiment setup and testing.

Project focus

The work aims to measure touch location and pressure with a compliant sensor that has connections exiting from one side.
