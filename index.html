01 / Selected work

Things I've built.

From soft tactile sensors to assistive technology and autonomous systems, these are some of the engineering projects I've loved working on.

Research · Soft robotics

Soft Tactile Sensor Research

At UCSC's Tactile Manipulation Lab, I designed and fabricated flexible sensors using a sacrificial PVA scaffold and a diamond-lattice channel structure. I investigated conductive materials and evaluated the sensors' response to applied pressure.

Sensor Design3D PrintingMaterials Testing
Read more about this project

Working with Professor Tae Myung Huh, I explored carbon black and silicone composites, conductive hydrogels, and geometric optimization. The goal was a soft sensing surface that can detect pressure and spatial touch information for robotic manipulation.

Visit the Tactile Manipulation Lab ↗

Graduate research · Biomedical devices

Noninvasive Metabolic Health Monitoring

At UC Berkeley's Liwei Lin Lab, I'm contributing to the development of a smartwatch that uses sweat sensing to monitor metabolic health. My work spans PCB debugging, sensor experiments, CAD, and automation for collecting training data.

Biomedical SensingPCB TestingExperimental Automation
Read more about this project

Our team is developing repeatable sweat measurement workflows and an automated system to help gather larger datasets for machine learning analysis. The research also includes smartwatch design and collaboration with UCSF toward human sample testing.

Visit the Liwei Lin Lab ↗

Senior capstone · Medical robotics

ProMotion Prosthetic Suspension

My senior capstone team developed an adaptive ankle-foot suspension system for transtibial prostheses. We integrated an adjustable air shock, mechanical linkages, an ESP32, IMU sensing, and servo actuation into a functional prototype.

Mechanical DesignCADMechatronics
Read more about this project

I focused on mechanical design, CAD, component selection, integration, and prototype fabrication. I loved that we could apply mechatronics to a device intended to make everyday mobility easier.

Read the ProMotion publication ↗

Mechatronics · Embedded systems

Mix-A-Tronic Bartender Robot

Designed and built an autonomous bartender robot that selects, mixes, and dispenses drinks. Our multidisciplinary team integrated fluid actuators, electronic controls, sensors, and embedded firmware into an interactive system.

Embedded SystemsFeedback ControlPrototyping
Read more about this project

We used sensor feedback and iterative testing to improve pouring consistency. It was one of my favorite ways to combine creativity, mechanical design, electronics, and programming into a complete working build.

02 / About me

Curious by nature.
Driven to build.

I've always loved understanding how things move, work, and interact with the world around them. What excites me most is seeing an idea go from a sketch to a real prototype, especially when mechanical design, electronics, and controls all come together.

I graduated with honors from UC Santa Cruz in 2026 with a B.S. in Robotics Engineering and a minor in Electrical Engineering. I'm now pursuing my Master of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, specializing in the control of robotic and autonomous systems.

My interests include robotics, biomedical technology, autonomous systems, and hands-on experimentation. Outside of the lab, I compete in figure skating and coach students of all levels. Both engineering and skating have taught me the value of patience, precision, and the willingness to keep improving.

03 / Beyond engineering

Life on the ice.

Figure skating has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. I grew up training competitively in Los Angeles, worked on triple jumps, and participated in U.S. Figure Skating National Showcase and international Theatre on Ice competitions.

Today, I continue competing as a collegiate skater at UC Berkeley while working as a professional figure skating coach. Since 2023, I've taught everyone from first-time skaters to competitive athletes learning double jumps.

Coaching has shaped how I lead and communicate, and skating has shaped how I handle challenges. To me, skating and engineering share a lot: mechanics, creativity, discipline, and countless small improvements that add up to something exciting.
