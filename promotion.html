ProMotion Prosthetic Suspension

UC Santa Cruz · Senior Capstone

Overview

Our UCSC senior capstone team developed ProMotion, a dynamically adjustable suspension system for below-knee prostheses. The prototype combines an air shock, mechanical linkage, displacement sensing, an IMU, servos, and an ESP32 controller.

My work

Mechanical design, CAD, shop fabrication, prototyping and system integration.

Project focus

We explored gait phase detection using acceleration and shock displacement, with the goal of adjusting compression and rebound damping for changing activity and terrain.

Project photos

Project poster and testing

Our poster summarizes the CAD assembly, gait detection state machine, testing approach, and results. The team reported reliable step and impeded-gait detection, target shock compression, displacement sensing error below 3%, and full servo actuation range.
