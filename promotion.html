ProMotion Prosthetic Suspension

UC Santa Cruz · Senior Capstone

Overview

Developed a prosthetic ankle-foot suspension prototype with an adjustable pneumatic shock, mechanical linkages, ESP32 controller, IMU and servos. I contributed to mechanical design, CAD, fabrication and system integration.

My work

Mechanical design, CAD, shop fabrication, prototyping and system integration.

Project focus

Built and presented a physical prototype as a team, with a focus on adaptivity across different walking conditions.
