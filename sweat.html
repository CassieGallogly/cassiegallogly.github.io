Wearable Sweat Sensing

UC Berkeley · Liwei Lin Laboratory

Overview

Graduate research supporting noninvasive metabolic monitoring using sweat sensing. The work includes PCB testing and debugging, investigating synthetic sweat conditions, and planning automated measurements to collect more consistent experimental data.

My work

Electronics debugging, experimental testing, CAD, hardware integration, optimization and data collection.

Project focus

The project explores how wearable sensing and data analysis can support metabolic health monitoring. This research is ongoing, with a UCSF collaboration for human testing.
